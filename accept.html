Problems are uncommon, but if one ever happens, here's exactly what to do.

If an alarm is faulty, or sounds or chirps for no obvious reason
  1. 1
    Get in touchCall 0475 184 984 or reply to your quote email. Tell us which alarm and what it's doing, and send a photo if you can.
  2. 2
    We post you a replacement, freeIf the alarm is faulty, we send a replacement at no cost, and postage is on us.
  3. 3
    Swap it and pair itSwap the alarm over. For hardwired alarms our electrician would have already wired the base plate, so you only change the alarm itself and never touch the wiring. Our step-by-step pairing guide shows how to connect it to your other alarms.
Safety first: if an alarm sounds and you can see or smell smoke, treat it as real: get everyone out and call 000. Otherwise, a sounding or chirping alarm is usually steam, dust or a low battery, and we'll help you work out which.
Not comfortable, or something doesn't look right? If the base plate looks damaged, you can see exposed wires, or you'd rather not do it yourself, leave it alone and call us. We'll arrange a licensed electrician. If the problem is with our installation work, we always fix it.
