Having trouble connecting an alarm?

1. Make sure all alarms are switched on. Hardwired units must be powered and installed by a qualified electrician.

2. Factory-reset the device that did not connect: hold its TEST button until its correct coloured indicator flashes 4 times, then release.

3. Put the master back into code sending mode: hold TEST until its indicator flashes 3 times, then release.

4. On the failed device, hold TEST until its indicator flashes 2 times, then release. Listen for a confirmation beep. A freshly reset battery alarm may connect automatically.

5. Finish by pressing TEST once on the master. Then test every alarm and, if included, the remote. Each test should sound all connected alarms.

Important: Hold the button continuously until you see the required number of flashes. Do not tap repeatedly. Pairing mode lasts about one minute, so if it times out you must re-enter it. Do not treat this guide as proof of regulatory compliance or as a substitute for manufacturer's instructions.
