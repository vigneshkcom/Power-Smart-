Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

PowerSmart (ABN 41 669 943 720) ("PowerSmart", "we", "us") supplies and installs smoke alarms in Queensland. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy applies to smokealarms.powersmartco.com.au and to our advertising on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram).

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, for example when you request a quote, call us, or book an installation:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and postcode;
  • Your property address and details about your home (such as number of bedrooms and storeys) needed to quote and install alarms;
  • Records of our communications with you, and payment and invoice details (card payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store your card number).

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your device, browser, pages viewed and the ad you clicked, using cookies and similar technologies.

2. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses the Meta Pixel. The Pixel sends information to Meta about how you interact with our site (for example, page views and when you submit a quote request) so that we can measure and improve our advertising and show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you under its own Privacy Policy.

If you submit your details through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we receive the information you provide in that form and use it only to respond to your enquiry.

You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Instagram Ad Preferences and you can block cookies in your browser settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you, provide quotes and book, perform and follow up installations;
  • To issue invoices, process payments and provide compliance certificates and warranty support;
  • To meet our legal and licensing obligations;
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and measure advertising performance;
  • To send you service-related messages, and marketing where permitted. You can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as licensed electricians and installers, payment processing (Stripe), email and SMS delivery, hosting and database providers, and advertising and analytics providers (including Meta). We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law. Some of these providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, after which we delete or de-identify it.

6. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below. We will respond within a reasonable time. To remove data we received via Meta, you can also contact us and we will delete it on request.

7. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handled your information, contact us and we will investigate and respond. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date above.
